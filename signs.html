How to place it in game
  1. You need the ComfySigns mod. Curved text is far over the 50 character vanilla limit.
  2. Press Copy sign text, place a sign, and paste the text into it.
  3. The letters will stack down one side of the sign. That's expected.
  4. Turn the sign on its side, a quarter turn (90°), so the stack of letters runs left to right. With ComfyGizmo, use its roll rotation to tip the sign over. The letters stand up and form an arch.
  5. If the arch is upside down, turn the sign the other way.
  6. To tweak it: Angle curve and Degrees per letter change how curved it is, Line height changes the space between letters, and Starting indent slides the whole arch along the sign.
