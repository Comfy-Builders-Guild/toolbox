Mods for builders

All of these are approved on Comfy. Links open a search on the mod site so you always land on the newest version.

Tip: a mod manager like r2modman, Thunderstore, or Gale makes installing and updating these a lot easier.

Building tools

ChestSnap Thunderstore ↗Adds snap points to chests. ComfyGizmo Thunderstore ↗Rotate pieces in any direction, not just side to side.Remove any old Gizmo you installed by hand first. EulersRuler Thunderstore ↗Shows a piece's health, stability and rotation as you build. FenceSnap Nexus ↗Adds snap points to fences and sharp stakes. GetOffMyLawn Thunderstore ↗Stronger build pieces, and you can ignore stability. InteractWhileBuilding Thunderstore ↗Open doors and chests without putting your hammer away. SearsCatalog Thunderstore ↗Resize and move the build menu.Turn off ComfyBuildExpansion if you have it. SnapPointsByComfy Thunderstore ↗Cycle through snap points without aiming right at them. StructurallyImpossible Thunderstore ↗Pieces you place ignore stability for good.Needs Unstrippable to work. Unstrippable Thunderstore ↗Keeps the extra data some mods save on pieces. StructurallyImpossible needs it.

Color and finishing touches

ColorfulLights Thunderstore ↗Color torches, fire pits, hearths and bonfires. ColorfulPieces Thunderstore ↗Color any build piece. ColorfulPortals Thunderstore ↗Color your portals. ColorfulWards Thunderstore ↗Color your wards. ComfySigns Thunderstore ↗White sign text by default, font choices, and room for way more text. TorchesAndResin Thunderstore ↗Torches start full and stay lit.

More to build with

Item Stand All Items Nexus ↗Put almost any item on an item stand. Pathside Assistance Thunderstore ↗Extra path and cultivator options for natural looking ground. PlantThings Thunderstore ↗More plants for gardens and landscaping.Only people with the mod can see them. PotteryBarn Thunderstore ↗Extra pieces in the hammer, like big floors and marble pieces. SkyTree Thunderstore ↗Walk and build on Yggdrasil.You still need a way up there.

Planning and checking

Hygge Thunderstore ↗Shows comfort values in the build menu. ZoneScouter Thunderstore ↗Shows sector borders and instance counts.

Sign helpers

Comfy Sign Builder Site ↗Turn pictures into sign art. Made by Kyris. Gradient Generator Site ↗Pick two colors and get every step in between. Unicode Symbols Site ↗Find stars, arrows, runes and other symbols to paste on signs. Unity Gradient Maker Site ↗Builds gradient text with the color tags already in place.
